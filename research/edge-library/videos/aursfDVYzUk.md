# aursfDVYzUk — The perfect trade entry with THIS volume analysis!
Channel: Tom Vorwald ("Trade the Traders"). URL: https://youtu.be/aursfDVYzUk
Gist: a teaching talk on volume profile (value area, point of control, low volume nodes) as places to look for trades. No entry trigger is ever given.

## 2. TESTABLE RULES
Nothing code-able as a setup. No market, timeframe, time window, entry trigger, stop, or target. He says the actual trigger comes from "a setup" taught in other lessons.
Building blocks only (definitions we could compute from tick or 1-min volume):
- Volume profile: total volume traded at each price over a chosen span (a day for day trading; also week or month).
- POC = price with the most volume. Value area = prices holding about 70% of volume (he also says 68.2%); edges are VAH and VAL.
- LVN = price zones with very little volume ("unfair price").
- VAGUE: how the value area is grown around the POC; price bin size; RTH vs full session; look-back span; what counts as a "reaction"; direction to trade after a touch.
Claims that could become hypotheses (our wording; he gives no rule and no test): price outside the value area tends to return to it or to the POC while the market is "stable" (he says about 70% of the time); breakouts from inside the value area make no sense without news; look for counter-trades or pullback entries at VAH, VAL, POC.

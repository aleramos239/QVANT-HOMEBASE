## 2. EVERY IDEA AND STRATEGY

1. Market model: range most of the time [3:02]-[4:03]

2. The zone and the 3-touch rule (the setup behind every trade) [4:03], [19:19]-[20:19], [27:27]-[28:28]

   - Setup: a big up or down move, then price goes sideways. Mark the top and bottom of the sideways part.
   - The zone only counts after the third touch (his third confirmation). VAGUE: touches of one edge or of both edges, and how close a touch must be.
   - He waits long on purpose: fewer false breaks, but he sometimes misses the move [20:19].
   - A good zone needs half a day to a full day on the 15-minute chart [21:19].
   - Exact drawing rules, and the rules to widen or shrink a zone when price makes new highs and lows inside it: SECRET, not given [4:03], [20:19]-[21:19], [33:31].
   - If the range looks messy he makes no trade [21:19].
   - The same three touches are needed for range trades and for breakout trades [28:28].

3. Range trade: fade the zone edges [12:13]-[13:14], [17:17]
   - Entry: limit order at the bottom of the zone (long) or the top (short). Only once the range is proven (see idea 2). Before that he calls it too early, because it is not yet known to be a range.
   - Target: the other side of the zone. Normally no partial exits, but he has no strict rule on it.
   - Stop: behind a nearby low or high if there is one. If there is nothing to lean on, he sizes the stop from the target so reward is over 3 times risk. Example: 200 points of target, stop of 30-40 points.
   - Filter: wide zones suit this trade. Stop below the zone, target the far side [17:17].
   - Time window, max trades a day: VAGUE.

4. Stacked zones [17:17]
   - Two zones sitting at the same price level make the range trade better. The only reason given is that it works better. No rule beyond that. VAGUE.

5. Breakout with confirmation (zone break, retest, go) [8:08]-[9:08], [16:15]-[17:17], [27:27]
   - Works both ways (he calls the down version a breakout too).
   - Trigger, long: one or two candles close out of the top of the zone, price comes back to the top edge of the zone, then a candle closes higher than the breakout candle. He does not chase: if price runs without the retest he is not in.
   - Entry: mostly a limit order at the retest level, sometimes a market order.
   - Stop: at the low of the zone when nothing else is there, so stop size is about the zone height [16:15]-[17:17].
   - Target: the next zone in the way. If there is none, see idea 11. Or trail, see idea 12.
   - Filter: best when the move before the zone is huge and the zone is small (he says reward to risk around 8). If the zone is wide and reward to risk is under about 2 he skips the breakout and trades the range instead [16:15]-[17:17].
   - Skip when: just before important news, or around 2:00 in the afternoon (his local time, likely Central Europe; he says the market often turns then) [26:27]. Something about oil here is garbled.
   - He says he takes nearly every 3-touch break, long and short, unless it is late in the day [27:27].
   - Max trades a day: VAGUE (he only says there are not many trades).

6. Breakout confirmation, said the second way: second lower low [14:14]-[15:15]
   - Down version: price breaks out of the zone and makes a low, pulls back, then breaks that low. The break of the first low is the entry signal.
   - The pullback matters a lot to him: it is his filter against false breaks.
   - Likely the same pattern as idea 5 seen from the short side, but here the trigger is a break of the first post-break low, not a close above the breakout candle. Kept separate because the trigger differs.
   - Stop, target: not said here. VAGUE.

7. Failed break and reclaim (he calls it the perfect trade) [9:08]-[10:09]
   - Trigger, long: price drops below the bottom of the zone, then a candle closes back inside the zone.
   - Entry: at the reclaim (the host points at a circle on the drawing). Order type VAGUE.
   - Stop: very small, just under the low of the failed break.
   - Target: do not stop at the top of the zone. Hold for the whole move up. With several contracts he takes about 30% off (one of three) at the top of the zone and holds the rest. He trades in multiples of three contracts for this.
   - Logic he gives: price tried to go down, had too little power, so it goes up. He admits he does not really know why.
   - Short mirror (break above the zone, close back inside, short): he does it sometimes but does not like it. Up-breaks carry power and the odds of a drop are worse [11:12]. So: long side preferred.
   - Depends on what the session before did [11:12]-[12:13]. VAGUE.

8. Counter move back to the start of the impulse (his favourite, zone to zone) [5:06]-[7:08]
   - Setup: big move up, then a zone (a P).
   - Trigger: price comes down out of the bottom of the zone. He goes short there. Exact trigger VAGUE (likely the confirmed break of idea 5 or 6).
   - Stop: just above the entry area, or above the whole zone.
   - Targets: first at the top edge of the zone below, second inside or at the far side of that zone (he watches what price does there), last at the start of the whole up-move. Reward to risk about 3 or more.
   - He says the trend-following break upward is the better and easier trade, but he feels better with this one. The return usually runs fast.
   - Claim: this counter-trend style has a slightly higher win rate for him [39:39].

9. Fast return to the origin after two zones [30:29]
   - When price has stepped up through two P zones and then comes back fast to the start of the move, he likes to take a trade there.
   - VAGUE: direction (riding the drop or buying at the origin), entry, stop, target. Pointed at on the drawing.

10. Europe-morning zone, US-open test of its low [23:22]-[24:24]

    - He uses this to change or close a running trade. He says there is no fixed system to it.
    - As a stand-alone rule: buy after the US open takes out the Europe-session low and turns back up. Entry, stop, target: VAGUE.

11. Measured-move target when there is no zone ahead [30:29]-[32:31]

    - Two follow-up remarks (something about doubling, and about managing the trade tightly) are garbled. VAGUE.
    - He normally always has a take-profit order.

12. Trail the stop under each new zone [18:17]-[19:19], [31:31]

    - Sometimes used with no target at all [31:31]. He has several rules for moving stops: not given.

22. Loss-streak rules [45:44]-[47:44]

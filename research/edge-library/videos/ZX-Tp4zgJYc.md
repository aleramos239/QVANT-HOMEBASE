2. Breakout patterns: wedges, triangles, rectangles after a consolidation [0:00-1:00].
3. Reversal patterns: double and triple tops, head and shoulders, cup and handle [1:00].
4. Elliott wave: five waves then three; wave 4 pulls back to 0.382 [1:00-2:02].
5. Fair value gap: the gap between the wicks beside a big candle; price may revisit it [2:02-3:03].
6. Candlestick patterns: engulfing (momentum), hammer and shooting star (rejection), doji (neutral) [3:03].
7. Heikin Ashi candles: colour = trend, body size = strength; not real prices [3:03-4:04].
8. Moon phases: new moon bullish, full moon bearish [4:04].
9. Renko blocks: a block per fixed price change [4:04-5:04].
10. Harmonic patterns (bat, butterfly, crab): Fibonacci ratios between four moves [5:04-6:04].
11. Support and resistance; dynamic support (a moving average); trend lines [6:04-7:04].
12. Gann angles [7:04-8:05].
13. Momentum indicators: MACD cross, moving average, parabolic SAR, Supertrend [8:05].
14. Oscillators: RSI and stochastic, oversold = up, overbought = down; best in sideways markets [8:05-9:05].
15. Divergence: price makes a higher high, the indicator a lower high = sell [9:05].
16. Volume: volume bars, VWAP as a moving support, volume profile levels [9:05-10:06].

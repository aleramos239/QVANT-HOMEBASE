## 7. RED FLAGS

- The win rate has no sample, no period, no trade list. The title number is a claim only.
- Many levels to choose from: FVG, inner high / low, CISD, wick, each on six timeframes (3m to 4h). Some level is near price on almost any day, so the picks shown are easy to fit after the fact.
- The two new level types were added after viewers said his Tuesday trade had no valid level. That is a rule made to fit a trade already taken. [26:21] [28:23]
- Bias is a judgment call. No rule for conflicting timeframes.
- Stop place "varies" (swing, body, gap edge, order block). The entry gap timeframe also varies (30 seconds to 5 minutes).
- He overrides his own rules by feel: skips a valid signal, takes a 30-second entry he tells beginners to avoid, re-enters after a stop, trails.
- Break-even and trailing change the real numbers. A break-even stop is likely not counted as a loss in the 70%.
- All examples are from a three-month run at all-time highs, and he says he mostly only buys. The result may be the up-trend, not the setup.
- Homework is "backtest one week". Far too small.
- The video ends with a paid mentorship pitch and student payout stories.
- The parts that carry the setup (higher-timeframe gaps, the inversion close, CISD, swing stop, level target, break-even) are the parts the pipeline cannot run.
- The repo already has several fvg runs and one failed fvg round on file (fvg_delta_nq, fvg_first / latest / cancel). A plain fvg card from this video would likely repeat them.

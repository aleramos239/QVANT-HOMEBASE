nq_fvg15_wick_out red on average

NQ, nyam 1m, family fvg_tap_disp. hm40 in-sample 3:1 months only (2021-10 to 2026-08). Test months NOT read. Nothing picked.
What it does
15-minute fair value gaps (overnight ones count). Price taps into a live gap on the 1-minute chart; then a 1-minute displacement candle (it closes past the previous 1-minute candle's wick and its body is at least k x the previous candle's body, k = 1.5 / 2 / 3) pushes back the gap's way and the 1-minute candle must CLOSE BEYOND the gap (above a bullish gap, below a bearish one) -> market entry with the gap. A 15-minute candle that closes through the gap (inversion) kills it: no trade. Own stop = the low (high) of the tap. Each gap trades once; entries 09:30-15:00 ET, up to 3 a day, flat 15:58.
Why it should work
A 15-minute fair value gap marks orders left unfilled; when price taps back into it and a 1-minute candle closes past the previous candle's wick with at least twice its body, beyond the gap, those orders are defending the gap and price should keep going its way.
Who loses: Traders who sold into a bullish gap (bought into a bearish gap) expecting it to break, trapped when the displacement candle closes back out.  |  When it should lose: Strong trend days that run straight through gaps: the bounce is only a pause and the gap gets closed through after the entry.

NQ, nyam 5m, family fvg_tap_disp. hm40 in-sample 3:1 months only (2021-10 to 2026-08). Test months NOT read. Nothing picked.
What it does
30- and 60-minute fair value gaps (overnight ones count; one trade at a time). Price taps into a live gap on the 5-minute chart; then a 5-minute displacement candle (body at least k x the 1-minute ATR, k = 1 / 1.5 / 2) pushes back the gap's way and the 1-minute candle must CLOSE BEYOND the gap (above a bullish gap, below a bearish one) -> market entry with the gap. A gap-size candle that closes through the gap (inversion) kills it: no trade. Own stop = the low (high) of the tap. Each gap trades once; entries 18:00-15:00 ET, up to 3 a day, flat 15:58.
Why it should work
A 30-minute or 1-hour fair value gap marks orders left unfilled; when price taps back into it at any time from 18:00 the evening before to 15:00 and a strong 5-minute candle closes back out beyond it, those orders are defending the gap and price should keep going its way.
Who loses: Traders who sold into a bullish gap (bought into a bearish gap) expecting it to break, trapped when the displacement candle closes back out.  |  When it should lose: Strong trend days that run straight through gaps, and thin overnight hours where small candles pass the test easily.

nq_fvg3060_d5_prev_g18_out red on average

NQ, nyam 5m, family fvg_tap_disp. hm40 in-sample 3:1 months only (2021-10 to 2026-08). Test months NOT read. Nothing picked.
What it does
30- and 60-minute fair value gaps (overnight ones count; one trade at a time). Price taps into a live gap on the 5-minute chart; then a 5-minute displacement candle (it closes past the previous 1-minute candle's wick and its body is at least k x the previous candle's body, k = 1.5 / 2 / 3) pushes back the gap's way and the 1-minute candle must CLOSE BEYOND the gap (above a bullish gap, below a bearish one) -> market entry with the gap. A gap-size candle that closes through the gap (inversion) kills it: no trade. Own stop = the low (high) of the tap. Each gap trades once; entries 18:00-15:00 ET, up to 3 a day, flat 15:58.
Why it should work
A 30-minute or 1-hour fair value gap marks orders left unfilled; when price taps back into it at any time from 18:00 the evening before to 15:00 and a 5-minute candle closes past the previous candle's wick with a bigger body, beyond the gap, those orders are defending the gap and price should keep going its way.
Who loses: Traders who sold into a bullish gap (bought into a bearish gap) expecting it to break, trapped when the displacement candle closes back out.  |  When it should lose: Strong trend days that run straight through gaps, and thin overnight hours where small candles pass the test easily.

nq_fvg3060_d5_prev_g18_in red on average

NQ, nyam 5m, family fvg_tap_disp. hm40 in-sample 3:1 months only (2021-10 to 2026-08). Test months NOT read. Nothing picked.
What it does
30- and 60-minute fair value gaps (overnight ones count; one trade at a time). Price taps into a live gap on the 5-minute chart; then a 5-minute displacement candle (it closes past the previous 1-minute candle's wick and its body is at least k x the previous candle's body, k = 1.5 / 2 / 3) pushes back the gap's way and the 1-minute candle may close INSIDE the gap or beyond it -> market entry with the gap. A gap-size candle that closes through the gap (inversion) kills it: no trade. Own stop = the low (high) of the tap. Each gap trades once; entries 18:00-15:00 ET, up to 3 a day, flat 15:58.
Why it should work
A 30-minute or 1-hour fair value gap marks orders left unfilled; when price taps back into it at any time from 18:00 the evening before to 15:00 and a 5-minute candle closes past the previous candle's wick with a bigger body, inside or beyond the gap, those orders are defending the gap and price should keep going its way.
Who loses: Traders who sold into a bullish gap (bought into a bearish gap) expecting it to break, trapped when the displacement candle closes back up.  |  When it should lose: Strong trend days that run straight through gaps, and thin overnight hours where small candles pass the test easily.

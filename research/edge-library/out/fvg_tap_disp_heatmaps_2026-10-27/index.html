nq_fvg15_tap_disp_out red on average

NQ, nyam 1m, family fvg_tap_disp. hm40 in-sample 3:1 months only (2021-10 to 2026-08). Test months NOT read. Nothing picked.
What it does
15-minute fair value gaps (overnight ones count). Price taps into a live gap on the 1-minute chart; then a 1-minute displacement candle (body at least k x the 1-minute ATR, k = 1 / 1.5 / 2) pushes back the gap's way and the 1-minute candle must CLOSE BEYOND the gap (above a bullish gap, below a bearish one) -> market entry with the gap. A 15-minute candle that closes through the gap (inversion) kills it: no trade. Own stop = the low (high) of the tap. Each gap trades once; entries 09:30-15:00 ET, up to 3 a day, flat 15:58.
Why it should work
A 15-minute fair value gap marks orders left unfilled; when price taps back into it and a strong 1-minute candle closes back out beyond it, those orders are defending the gap and price should keep going its way.
Who loses: Traders who sold into a bullish gap (bought into a bearish gap) expecting it to break, trapped when the displacement candle closes back out.  |  When it should lose: Strong trend days that run straight through gaps: the bounce is only a pause and the gap gets closed through after the entry.

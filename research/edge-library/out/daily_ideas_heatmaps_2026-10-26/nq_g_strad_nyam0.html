nq_g_strad_nyam0 red on average

NQ, nyam 5m, family straddle. hm40 in-sample 3:1 months only (2021-10 to 2026-08). Test months NOT read. Nothing picked.
What it does
At 09:30 ET it places a buy-stop above and a sell-stop below the price, each 0.25/0.5/1.0 x ATR away. The first one to fill is the trade, the other is cancelled. One trade a day, 5-minute bars, flat 15:58.
Why it should work
Stops either side of price at 09:30: the first to fill is the day's side for that window.
Who loses: Range traders who sell/buy the first push.  |  When it should lose: Quiet drift.

nq_d43_sma_nyam5 red on average

NQ, nyam 5m, family win_dir. hm40 in-sample 3:1 months only (2021-10 to 2026-08). Test months NOT read. Nothing picked.
What it does
Once a day, at 09:35 ET, it buys or sells at market with 5-minute bars. The side comes from yesterday's close versus the average of the last 3/5/10 daily closes (above = long). It trades the way that points. No signal (flat, or data missing) = no trade. It then holds until the stop, the target, or 15:58 ET.
Why it should work
Short-term trend (prior close above its n-day mean) carries into the next morning.
Who loses: Traders who fade the short-term trend.  |  When it should lose: Choppy weeks with no trend.

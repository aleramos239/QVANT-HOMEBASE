nq_d46_first30_follow_nyam5 mixed

NQ, nyam 5m, family win_dir. hm40 in-sample 3:1 months only (2021-10 to 2026-08). Test months NOT read. Nothing picked.
What it does
Once a day, at 10:00 / 10:15 / 10:30 ET (three entry times tried), it buys or sells at market with 5-minute bars. The side comes from the price move from 09:30 to 10:00 ET (up = long, down = short). It trades the way that points. No signal (flat, or data missing) = no trade. It then holds until the stop, the target, or 15:58 ET.
Why it should work
The first 30 minutes show who is in control; the next hours carry on with it.
Who loses: Traders who fade the opening move.  |  When it should lose: Opening spikes that reverse by 10:30.

nq_e74_delta15_nyam5 mixed

NQ, nyam 5m, family win_dir. hm40 in-sample 3:1 months only (2021-10 to 2026-08). Test months NOT read. Nothing picked.
What it does
Once a day, at 10:00 / 10:15 / 10:30 ET (three entry times tried), it buys or sells at market with 5-minute bars. The side comes from net buyer-minus-seller volume from 09:30 to 09:45 (more buying = long). It trades the way that points. No signal (flat, or data missing) = no trade. It then holds until the stop, the target, or 12:00 ET.
Why it should work
The net aggressor volume of the first 15 minutes sets the side for 10:00 to 12:00.
Who loses: Passive traders who fade the aggressors.  |  When it should lose: Absorbed flow.

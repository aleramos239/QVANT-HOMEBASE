nq_d51_day_fade_pm5 red on average

NQ, pm 5m, family win_dir. hm40 in-sample 3:1 months only (2021-10 to 2026-08). Test months NOT read. Nothing picked.
What it does
Once a day, at 14:00 / 14:15 / 14:30 ET (three entry times tried), it buys or sells at market with 5-minute bars. The side comes from price now versus the 09:30 open (above = long). It trades AGAINST what that points to (a fade). No signal (flat, or data missing) = no trade. It then holds until the stop, the target, or 15:58 ET.
Why it should work
An extended day reverts into the close as profit-taking and inventory cleanup arrive.
Who loses: Late trend chasers.  |  When it should lose: Trend days into the close.

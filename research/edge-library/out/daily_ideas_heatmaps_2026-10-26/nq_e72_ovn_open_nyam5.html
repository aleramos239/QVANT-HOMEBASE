nq_e72_ovn_open_nyam5 red on average

NQ, nyam 5m, family win_dir. hm40 in-sample 3:1 months only (2021-10 to 2026-08). Test months NOT read. Nothing picked.
What it does
Once a day, at 10:00 / 10:15 / 10:30 ET (three entry times tried), it buys or sells at market with 5-minute bars. The side comes from the 09:30-10:00 move, only on days when it points the same way as the overnight gap (otherwise no trade). It trades the way that points. No signal (flat, or data missing) = no trade. It then holds until the stop, the target, or 15:58 ET.
Why it should work
A close on the same side of the 09:30 open as the overnight move confirms it; join it.
Who loses: Traders who fade the overnight move.  |  When it should lose: Days the overnight move is undone.

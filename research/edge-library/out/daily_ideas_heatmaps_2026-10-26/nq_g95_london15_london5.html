nq_g95_london15_london5 red on average

NQ, london 5m, family win_bracket. hm40 in-sample 3:1 months only (2021-10 to 2026-08). Test months NOT read. Nothing picked.
What it does
Marks the high and low from 03:00 to 03:05/03:15/03:30 ET (the range). After it closes, a buy-stop sits just above the high and a sell-stop just below the low; the first one hit is the day's trade, the other is cancelled. One trade a day inside London (03:00-08:25), 5-minute bars, flat 15:58.
Why it should work
The first minutes of London set its range; the first break sets the session's side.
Who loses: Range fade traders in Europe.  |  When it should lose: False breaks.

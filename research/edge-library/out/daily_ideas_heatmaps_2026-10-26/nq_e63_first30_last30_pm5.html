nq_e63_first30_last30_pm5 red on average

NQ, pm 5m, family win_dir. hm40 in-sample 3:1 months only (2021-10 to 2026-08). Test months NOT read. Nothing picked.
What it does
Once a day, at 15:30 ET, it buys or sells at market with 5-minute bars. The side comes from the price move from 09:30 to 09:45/10:00/10:30 ET (up = long, down = short). It trades the way that points. No signal (flat, or data missing) = no trade. It then holds until the stop, the target, or 15:58 ET.
Why it should work
The first 30 minutes predict the last 30 (intraday momentum, Gao et al.): the same traders re-balance at the close.
Who loses: Late-day fade traders.  |  When it should lose: Closing reversals.

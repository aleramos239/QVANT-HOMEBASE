nq_f_noise_eve5 green on average

NQ, eve 5m, family noise_band_eve. hm40 in-sample 3:1 months only (2021-10 to 2026-08). Test months NOT read. Nothing picked.
What it does
Draws a band around the 18:00 evening open: open +/- k x the daily ATR (k = 0.2, 0.3, 0.4). Goes LONG when a 5-minute bar closes above the upper line AND above the VWAP since that open; SHORT when it closes below the lower line AND below VWAP. Only inside the evening before (18:00-23:59 ET). Up to 3 trades a day, either side, flat 23:55.
Why it should work
Price that leaves the open-anchored noise band with VWAP behind it shows a real move.
Who loses: Fade traders inside the band.  |  When it should lose: Noise days.

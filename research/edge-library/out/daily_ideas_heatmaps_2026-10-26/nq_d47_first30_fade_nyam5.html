nq_d47_first30_fade_nyam5 red on average

NQ, nyam 5m, family win_dir. hm40 in-sample 3:1 months only (2021-10 to 2026-08). Test months NOT read. Nothing picked.
What it does
Once a day, at 10:00 / 10:15 / 10:30 ET (three entry times tried), it buys or sells at market with 5-minute bars. The side comes from the price move from 09:30 to 10:00 ET (up = long, down = short). It trades AGAINST what that points to (a fade). No signal (flat, or data missing) = no trade. It then holds until the stop, the target, or 15:58 ET.
Why it should work
The first 30 minutes overshoot on stops and market orders; the rest of the morning gives it back.
Who loses: Traders who chase the open.  |  When it should lose: Trend days.

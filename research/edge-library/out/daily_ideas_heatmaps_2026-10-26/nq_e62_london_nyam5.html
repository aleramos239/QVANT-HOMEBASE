nq_e62_london_nyam5 red on average

NQ, nyam 5m, family win_dir. hm40 in-sample 3:1 months only (2021-10 to 2026-08). Test months NOT read. Nothing picked.
What it does
Once a day, at 09:30 ET, it buys or sells at market with 5-minute bars. The side comes from the price move from 03:00/05:00/07:00 to 08:25 ET (up = long, down = short). It trades the way that points. No signal (flat, or data missing) = no trade. It then holds until the stop, the target, or 15:58 ET.
Why it should work
London's move into the New York open shows the order flow that the US morning continues.
Who loses: Traders who fade London at the US open.  |  When it should lose: US data reversals.

nq_d58_trend_nyam5 red on average

NQ, nyam 5m, family win_dir. hm40 in-sample 3:1 months only (2021-10 to 2026-08). Test months NOT read. Nothing picked.
What it does
Once a day, at 09:30 ET, it buys or sells at market with 5-minute bars. The side comes from yesterday's close versus the average of the last 20/50/100 daily closes (above = long). It trades the way that points. No signal (flat, or data missing) = no trade. It then holds until the stop, the target, or 15:58 ET.
Why it should work
The daily trend (prior close vs its n-day mean) sets the morning's side, every day.
Who loses: Counter-trend traders.  |  When it should lose: Trend reversals.

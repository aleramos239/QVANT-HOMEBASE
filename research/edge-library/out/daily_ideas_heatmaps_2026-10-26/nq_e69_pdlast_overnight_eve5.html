nq_e69_pdlast_overnight_eve5 red on average

NQ, eve 5m, family win_dir. hm40 in-sample 3:1 months only (2021-10 to 2026-08). Test months NOT read. Nothing picked.
What it does
Once a day, at 19:15 / 19:30 / 20:00 ET (three entry times tried), it buys or sells at market with 5-minute bars. The side comes from yesterday's price move from 15:00 to 16:00 (up = long). It trades the way that points. No signal (flat, or data missing) = no trade. It then holds until the stop, the target, or 04:00 ET.
Why it should work
The prior day's last hour predicts the overnight session.
Who loses: Traders who fade the close.  |  When it should lose: Nights that reverse the close.

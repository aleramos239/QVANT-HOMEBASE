nq_e66_late_morn_mid5 red on average

NQ, mid 5m, family win_dir. hm40 in-sample 3:1 months only (2021-10 to 2026-08). Test months NOT read. Nothing picked.
What it does
Once a day, at 12:00 ET, it buys or sells at market with 5-minute bars. The side comes from the price move from 10:00/10:30/11:00 to 12:00 ET (up = long, down = short). It trades the way that points. No signal (flat, or data missing) = no trade. It then holds until the stop, the target, or 14:00 ET.
Why it should work
The late-morning side carries through the lunch lull to 14:00.
Who loses: Lunch-hour fade traders.  |  When it should lose: Lunch reversals.

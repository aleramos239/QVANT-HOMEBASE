nq_f84_wick_eve5 red on average

NQ, eve 5m, family candle_sweep. hm40 in-sample 3:1 months only (2021-10 to 2026-08). Test months NOT read. Nothing picked.
What it does
On 5-minute bars during the evening before (18:00-23:59 ET): goes LONG when a bar closes above the previous bar's high and SHORT when it closes below the previous bar's low, but only if the net aggressor volume (delta) of the 5 minutes before the signal points the same way and is at least as strong as the median of that same clock window over the last 20 days. Market order at the close, filled at the next bar's open. Up to 3 trades a day, flat 23:55. The 'back' setting (range / body / far) is not read in this mode, so its three rows are the same boxes.
Why it should work
A bar that breaks the previous bar's extreme with the prior bar's delta behind it shows aggressors in control.
Who loses: Traders on the other side of the break.  |  When it should lose: Thin chop.

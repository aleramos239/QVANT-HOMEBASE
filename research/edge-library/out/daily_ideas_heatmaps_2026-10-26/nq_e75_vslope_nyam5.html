nq_e75_vslope_nyam5 red on average

NQ, nyam 5m, family win_dir. hm40 in-sample 3:1 months only (2021-10 to 2026-08). Test months NOT read. Nothing picked.
What it does
Once a day, at 10:30 / 10:45 / 11:00 ET (three entry times tried), it buys or sells at market with 5-minute bars. The side comes from whether the VWAP rose or fell between 09:45 and the entry (rising = long). It trades the way that points. No signal (flat, or data missing) = no trade. It then holds until the stop, the target, or 12:30 ET.
Why it should work
A rising VWAP over the first hour means buyers keep paying up; the next two hours follow it.
Who loses: Fade traders against the VWAP trend.  |  When it should lose: Flat VWAP drift.

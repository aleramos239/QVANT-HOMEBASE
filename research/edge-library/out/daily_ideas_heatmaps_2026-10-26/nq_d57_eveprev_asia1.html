nq_d57_eveprev_asia1 red on average

NQ, asia 1m, family win_dir. hm40 in-sample 3:1 months only (2021-10 to 2026-08). Test months NOT read. Nothing picked.
What it does
Once a day, at 00:15 / 00:30 / 00:45 ET (three entry times tried), it buys or sells at market with 1-minute bars. The side comes from the price move from 18:00 to 23:59 ET (up = long, down = short). It trades the way that points. No signal (flat, or data missing) = no trade. It then holds until the stop, the target, or 08:25 ET.
Why it should work
The evening's side (18:00 to midnight) carries into the night and morning.
Who loses: Traders who fade the evening move.  |  When it should lose: Nights that reverse the evening.

nq_f_noise_nyam1 mixed

NQ, nyam 1m, family noise_band. hm40 in-sample 3:1 months only (2021-10 to 2026-08). Test months NOT read. Nothing picked.
What it does
Draws a band around the 09:30 open: open +/- k x the daily ATR (k = 0.2, 0.3, 0.4). Goes LONG when a 1-minute bar closes above the upper line AND above the VWAP since that open; SHORT when it closes below the lower line AND below VWAP. Only inside New York morning (09:30-11:00). Up to 3 trades a day, either side, flat 15:58.
Why it should work
A close outside the day's noise on the same side of VWAP shows one-way control (1-minute bars).
Who loses: Fade traders at the band.  |  When it should lose: Range days.

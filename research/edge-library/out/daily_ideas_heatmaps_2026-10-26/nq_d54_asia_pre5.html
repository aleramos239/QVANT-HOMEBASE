nq_d54_asia_pre5 red on average

NQ, pre 5m, family win_dir. hm40 in-sample 3:1 months only (2021-10 to 2026-08). Test months NOT read. Nothing picked.
What it does
Once a day, at 08:30 ET, it buys or sells at market with 5-minute bars. The side comes from the price move from 00:00 to 01:00/02:00/03:00 ET (up = long, down = short). It trades the way that points. No signal (flat, or data missing) = no trade. It then holds until the stop, the target, or 15:58 ET.
Why it should work
The Asian session's drift, read by 08:30, shows the overnight side; the data-release hour carries on with it.
Who loses: Traders who fade the overnight move.  |  When it should lose: Data surprises that reverse the night.

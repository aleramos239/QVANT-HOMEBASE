nq_d55_asia_london5 red on average

NQ, london 5m, family win_dir. hm40 in-sample 3:1 months only (2021-10 to 2026-08). Test months NOT read. Nothing picked.
What it does
Once a day, at 03:00 ET, it buys or sells at market with 5-minute bars. The side comes from the price move from 00:00/01:00/02:00 to 03:00 ET (up = long, down = short). It trades the way that points. No signal (flat, or data missing) = no trade. It then holds until the stop, the target, or 15:58 ET.
Why it should work
The Asian session return predicts the London session (the same side, as Europe joins).
Who loses: Traders who fade Asia into London.  |  When it should lose: London opening reversals.

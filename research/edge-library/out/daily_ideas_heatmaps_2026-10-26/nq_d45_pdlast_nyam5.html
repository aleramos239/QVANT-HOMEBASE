nq_d45_pdlast_nyam5 red on average

NQ, nyam 5m, family win_dir. hm40 in-sample 3:1 months only (2021-10 to 2026-08). Test months NOT read. Nothing picked.
What it does
Once a day, at 09:30 / 09:45 / 10:00 ET (three entry times tried), it buys or sells at market with 5-minute bars. The side comes from yesterday's price move from 15:00 to 16:00 (up = long). It trades the way that points. No signal (flat, or data missing) = no trade. It then holds until the stop, the target, or 15:58 ET.
Why it should work
The prior day's last hour shows where the closing money went; the next morning keeps that side.
Who loses: Traders who fade the close.  |  When it should lose: Mornings that reverse the close.

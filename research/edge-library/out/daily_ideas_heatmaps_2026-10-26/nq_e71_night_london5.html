nq_e71_night_london5 red on average

NQ, london 5m, family win_dir. hm40 in-sample 3:1 months only (2021-10 to 2026-08). Test months NOT read. Nothing picked.
What it does
Once a day, at 04:00 ET, it buys or sells at market with 5-minute bars. The side comes from the price move from 00:00/01:00/02:00 to 04:00 ET (up = long, down = short). It trades the way that points. No signal (flat, or data missing) = no trade. It then holds until the stop, the target, or 09:25 ET.
Why it should work
The Asia-to-London side between midnight and 04:00 carries to the New York pre-open.
Who loses: Overnight fade traders.  |  When it should lose: Pre-open reversals.

nq_e64_f30ovn_last30_pm5 red on average

NQ, pm 5m, family win_dir. hm40 in-sample 3:1 months only (2021-10 to 2026-08). Test months NOT read. Nothing picked.
What it does
Once a day, at 15:15 / 15:30 / 15:45 ET (three entry times tried), it buys or sells at market with 5-minute bars. The side comes from the 09:30-10:00 move, only on days when it points the same way as the overnight gap (otherwise no trade). It trades the way that points. No signal (flat, or data missing) = no trade. It then holds until the stop, the target, or 15:58 ET.
Why it should work
When the overnight move and the first 30 minutes agree, that side is the day's and it runs into the last 30 minutes.
Who loses: Late fade traders.  |  When it should lose: Days the two disagree (no trade) or the close reverses.

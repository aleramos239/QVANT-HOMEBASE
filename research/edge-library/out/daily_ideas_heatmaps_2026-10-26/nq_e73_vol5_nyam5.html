nq_e73_vol5_nyam5 red on average

NQ, nyam 5m, family win_dir. hm40 in-sample 3:1 months only (2021-10 to 2026-08). Test months NOT read. Nothing picked.
What it does
Once a day, at 09:35 / 09:45 / 10:00 ET (three entry times tried), it buys or sells at market with 5-minute bars. The side comes from the 09:30-09:35 move, kept as is when its volume is at least the 14-day average of that same window and flipped when it is quieter. It trades the way that points. No signal (flat, or data missing) = no trade. It then holds until the stop, the target, or 15:58 ET.
Why it should work
A loud first five minutes (volume at or above its 14-day mean) continues; a quiet one fades.
Who loses: Traders on the other side of a loud open.  |  When it should lose: Loud opens that reverse.

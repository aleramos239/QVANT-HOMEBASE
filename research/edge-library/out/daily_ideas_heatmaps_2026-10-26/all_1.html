nq_f_wick_nyam30 green on average

NQ, nyam 30m, family candle_sweep. hm40 in-sample 3:1 months only (2021-10 to 2026-08). Test months NOT read. Nothing picked.
What it does
On 30-minute bars during New York morning (09:30-11:00): goes LONG when a bar closes above the previous bar's high and SHORT when it closes below the previous bar's low, but only if the net aggressor volume (delta) of the 5 minutes before the signal points the same way and is at least as strong as the median of that same clock window over the last 20 days. Market order at the close, filled at the next bar's open. Up to 3 trades a day, flat 15:58. The 'back' setting (range / body / far) is not read in this mode, so its three rows are the same boxes.
Why it should work
A bar that breaks the previous bar's extreme with the prior bar's delta behind it shows aggressors in control.
Who loses: Traders on the other side of the break.  |  When it should lose: Thin chop.

NQ, nyam 5m, family win_bracket. hm40 in-sample 3:1 months only (2021-10 to 2026-08). Test months NOT read. Nothing picked.
What it does
Marks the high and low from 00:00/01:00/03:00 to 09:30 ET (the range). After it closes, a buy-stop sits just above the high and a sell-stop just below the low; the first one hit is the day's trade, the other is cancelled. One trade a day inside New York morning (09:30-11:00), 5-minute bars, flat 15:58.
Why it should work
The overnight high/low is the range the US open must resolve; the first side to break is the day's.
Who loses: Range fade traders at the open.  |  When it should lose: False breaks.

NQ, nyam 5m, family pd_bracket. hm40 in-sample 3:1 months only (2021-10 to 2026-08). Test months NOT read. Nothing picked.
What it does
At the start of New York morning (09:30-11:00) (+0/15/30 minutes) it places a buy-stop one tick above yesterday's high and a sell-stop one tick below yesterday's low (a side whose level price has already passed is not armed). First one hit trades, the stop is the other level. One trade a day, flat 15:58.
Why it should work
The prior day's high and low are the levels every desk watches; the first one broken sets the day's side.
Who loses: Range traders at yesterday's extremes.  |  When it should lose: False breaks of yesterday's levels.

NQ, nyam 5m, family win_dir. hm40 in-sample 3:1 months only (2021-10 to 2026-08). Test months NOT read. Nothing picked.
What it does
Once a day, at 10:30 ET, it buys or sells at market with 5-minute bars. The side comes from the price move from 09:30/09:45/10:00 to 10:30 ET (up = long, down = short). It trades the way that points. No signal (flat, or data missing) = no trade. It then holds until the stop, the target, or 12:00 ET.
Why it should work
The first hour sets the side for the next 90 minutes.
Who loses: Traders who fade the opening hour.  |  When it should lose: Morning reversals.

nq_d42_gap_fade_nyam5 mixed

NQ, nyam 5m, family win_dir. hm40 in-sample 3:1 months only (2021-10 to 2026-08). Test months NOT read. Nothing picked.
What it does
Once a day, at 09:30 / 09:45 / 10:00 ET (three entry times tried), it buys or sells at market with 5-minute bars. The side comes from where price is versus yesterday's 16:00 close (above = long, below = short). It trades AGAINST what that points to (a fade). No signal (flat, or data missing) = no trade. It then holds until the stop, the target, or 15:58 ET.
Why it should work
An overnight gap is an overreaction that the cash open takes back.
Who loses: Traders who chase the gap at the open.  |  When it should lose: Trend days that follow the gap.

nq_d56_prday_eve5 red on average

NQ, eve 5m, family win_dir. hm40 in-sample 3:1 months only (2021-10 to 2026-08). Test months NOT read. Nothing picked.
What it does
Once a day, at 19:30 / 20:00 / 20:30 ET (three entry times tried), it buys or sells at market with 5-minute bars. The side comes from yesterday's price move from 09:30 to 16:00 (up = long). It trades the way that points. No signal (flat, or data missing) = no trade. It then holds until the stop, the target, or 23:55 ET.
Why it should work
The prior cash day's side (09:30 to 16:00) carries into the evening.
Who loses: Traders who fade the cash day after the close.  |  When it should lose: Evenings that give the day back.

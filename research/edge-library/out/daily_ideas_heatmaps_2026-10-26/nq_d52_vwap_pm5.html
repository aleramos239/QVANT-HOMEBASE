nq_d52_vwap_pm5 mixed

NQ, pm 5m, family win_dir. hm40 in-sample 3:1 months only (2021-10 to 2026-08). Test months NOT read. Nothing picked.
What it does
Once a day, at 14:45 / 15:00 / 15:15 ET (three entry times tried), it buys or sells at market with 5-minute bars. The side comes from price now versus the day's VWAP (above = long). It trades the way that points. No signal (flat, or data missing) = no trade. It then holds until the stop, the target, or 15:58 ET.
Why it should work
The side of VWAP at 15:00 is the side the closing flows push.
Who loses: Fade traders at VWAP late.  |  When it should lose: Last-hour reversals.

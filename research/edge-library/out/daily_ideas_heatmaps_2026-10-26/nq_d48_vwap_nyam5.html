nq_d48_vwap_nyam5 red on average

NQ, nyam 5m, family win_dir. hm40 in-sample 3:1 months only (2021-10 to 2026-08). Test months NOT read. Nothing picked.
What it does
Once a day, at 10:15 / 10:30 / 10:45 ET (three entry times tried), it buys or sells at market with 5-minute bars. The side comes from price now versus the day's VWAP (above = long). It trades the way that points. No signal (flat, or data missing) = no trade. It then holds until the stop, the target, or 15:58 ET.
Why it should work
Price above (below) the day's VWAP at 10:30 means buyers (sellers) are paying up; the side holds.
Who loses: Fade traders at VWAP.  |  When it should lose: Chop around VWAP.

nq_g93_on_bracket_nyam5 green on average

NQ, nyam 5m, family win_bracket. hm40 in-sample 3:1 months only (2021-10 to 2026-08). Test months NOT read. Nothing picked.
What it does
Marks the high and low from 00:00/01:00/03:00 to 09:30 ET (the range). After it closes, a buy-stop sits just above the high and a sell-stop just below the low; the first one hit is the day's trade, the other is cancelled. One trade a day inside New York morning (09:30-11:00), 5-minute bars, flat 15:58.
Why it should work
The overnight high/low is the range the US open must resolve; the first side to break is the day's.
Who loses: Range fade traders at the open.  |  When it should lose: False breaks.

nq_f_noise_mid5 green on average

NQ, mid 5m, family noise_band. hm40 in-sample 3:1 months only (2021-10 to 2026-08). Test months NOT read. Nothing picked.
What it does
Draws a band around the 09:30 open: open +/- k x the daily ATR (k = 0.2, 0.3, 0.4). Goes LONG when a 5-minute bar closes above the upper line AND above the VWAP since that open; SHORT when it closes below the lower line AND below VWAP. Only inside midday (11:00-13:30). Up to 3 trades a day, either side, flat 15:58.
Why it should work
A close outside the day's noise (open +/- k x daily ATR) on the same side of VWAP shows one-way control.
Who loses: Fade traders at the band.  |  When it should lose: Range days.

What it does
Once a day, at 14:00 / 14:15 / 14:30 ET (three entry times tried), it buys or sells at market with 5-minute bars. The side comes from price now versus the 09:30 open (above = long). It trades the way that points. No signal (flat, or data missing) = no trade. It then holds until the stop, the target, or 15:58 ET.
Why it should work
The day's side at 14:00 (vs the 09:30 open) carries into the close as trend funds add.
Who loses: Late fade traders.  |  When it should lose: Afternoon reversals.

NQ, pm 5m, family win_dir. hm40 in-sample 3:1 months only (2021-10 to 2026-08). Test months NOT read. Nothing picked.
What it does
Once a day, at 14:00 ET, it buys or sells at market with 5-minute bars. The side comes from the price move from 11:00/12:00/12:30 to 14:00 ET (up = long, down = short). It trades the way that points. No signal (flat, or data missing) = no trade. It then holds until the stop, the target, or 15:58 ET.
Why it should work
The midday side carries into the afternoon session.
Who loses: Afternoon fade traders.  |  When it should lose: Afternoon reversals.

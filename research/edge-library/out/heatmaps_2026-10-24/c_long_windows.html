Settings: off = no delta check (idea A's baseline). Any = the net aggressor volume from the window's open (9:30, or 6 pm for the overnight window) to the start of the signal candle points the trade's way. Above median = it is also at least the median of that same clock window over the 20 earlier days. Top 20% = at least that window's 80th percentile. The window's first candle has no flow before it yet and does not trade unless the flow is off. Shading uses one scale per bar size, centred at $0.

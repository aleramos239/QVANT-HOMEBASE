nq_g94_first5_nyam5 green on average

NQ, nyam 5m, family win_bracket. hm40 in-sample 3:1 months only (2021-10 to 2026-08). Test months NOT read. Nothing picked.
What it does
Marks the high and low from 09:30 to 09:35/09:40/09:45 ET (the range). After it closes, a buy-stop sits just above the high and a sell-stop just below the low; the first one hit is the day's trade, the other is cancelled. One trade a day inside New York morning (09:30-11:00), 5-minute bars, flat 15:58.
Why it should work
The first minutes' high/low is the opening range; its first break sets the side.
Who loses: Opening range fade traders.  |  When it should lose: False breaks.

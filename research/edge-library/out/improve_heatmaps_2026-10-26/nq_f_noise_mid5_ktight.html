nq_f_noise_mid5_ktight green on average

NQ, mid 5m, family noise_band. hm40 in-sample 3:1 months only (2021-10 to 2026-08). Test months NOT read. Nothing picked.
What it does
Draws a band around the 09:30 open: open +/- k x the daily ATR (k = 0.1, 0.15, 0.2). Goes LONG when a 5-minute bar closes above the upper line AND above the VWAP since that open; SHORT when it closes below the lower line AND below VWAP. Only inside midday (11:00-13:30). Up to 3 trades a day, either side, flat 15:58.
Why it should work
Price that leaves the open-anchored noise band with VWAP behind it shows a real move.
Who loses: Fade traders at the band.  |  When it should lose: Range days.

Sit-down story video (filmed in Dubai). No chart, no slides. Few testable numbers; mostly his history and the "model it first" message.

## Summary
He started prop firms in Feb 2025 with $5,000 of evals and got $17,000 of payouts the first month ("my model said ~3.5x"). He then
reinvested everything (17k -> 50k -> 100k -> 200k of evals) and hit a $125k payout month mid-internship (June/July 2025). His
message: model the prop firm as a bounded random walk (+3k / -2k, solved like a boundary problem), size for the firm, keep risk of
ruin under 0.5 %, use a strategy that works at any hour (fair-price mean reversion), treat it as a side job until $10k profit.

## Numbers he states
- Start: $5,000 of evals -> $17,000 payouts in the first month (2025). Model predicted ~3.5x.
- Then all-in reinvestment: $17k of evals -> planned $50k -> $100k -> $200k; $125k payout month in summer 2025.
- Total: $2M payouts in ~2 years (also "less than 0.01 % of people" can do this).
- Ron (guest on his 2nd channel): $250k payouts from a $250 start, trading only Asia / overnight while working 7 AM-7 PM.
- **Risk of ruin = (chance an eval ends with no payout) ^ (evals you can buy).** Example: 80 % no-payout, 10 evals -> 0.8^10 = 10.7 %.
  Target: **risk of ruin below 0.5 %**.
- Example economics: "spend 100, maybe get 2,000 back"; 20 x $100 evals for one $2,000 payout = break-even.
- Hours vs income (his rough map): $10k/month ~2 h a day; $30k ~3 h; $100k ~6 h.

## Rules / method
- The eval is a two-bound problem: start 50K, -2K floor, +3K target -> probability of hitting either bound (differential equations,
  "boundary problems" from his degree).
- The firm makes money when you fail, so its rules will not suit a normal strategy: build the strategy AND the sizing for the prop
  rules, not for a live account.
- Strategy = mean reversion toward a fair value + a bias. "Based on how many points you have for your reversion, you trade a
  specific prop firm" (pick the firm/account by the points available to fair value: e.g. 100 points away -> a 100-point trade on the
  firm where that size fits best).
- Must work at any time of day: he traded NY AM when free, 18:00 and Asia on office days, plus some overnight trades; not London (3 AM).
  "One specific setup, NY AM, one trade a day ... not going to work ... not scalable."
- Steps to quit a job: (1) set an income target and hours; (2) model it (pass rate, payout rate, $ out per $ in, risk of ruin);
  (3) find trading windows you can actually make; (4) after the first $10k profit, decide whether to scale (more evals, more hours).
- Do not quit until the model is ~90 % certain; never go all in.

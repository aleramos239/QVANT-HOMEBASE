## Summary
$59k of payouts in the last 2 weeks, $1.828M total. Eval trade is always the same: **25 stop / 38 target (1:1.5)**; 50 / 76 when the
opening candle is over 25 pts; 12.5 / 19 with double contracts (same $ risk) when the room to fair is small. The room left to fair

break-of-structure signals, go to FUNDED accounts with targets near fair (56-150 pts); displacement signals go to EVALS. One signal
goes to one account type, never an eval and a funded on the same signal. ~20 trades a day; aim 10 in the NY AM.

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Eval trade | "I always do 38/25 as my evaluation trade, 1 to 1.5, best way to pass evals" |

| One signal, one type | never an eval and a funded on the same signal |
| Layering | adds go on evals; funded accounts are not copy traded |

| Continuation | only in the first 5 minutes of a session, only with a break of structure. Friday: 3 continuation shorts, all won |

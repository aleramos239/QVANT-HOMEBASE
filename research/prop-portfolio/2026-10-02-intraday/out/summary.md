1,781 configs = the pilot's 1,283 + 498 pass-2 configs the pilot had searched but never carried into its final set. Same rules grid, same code; only the breach model changed from 'realized' (closed balance) to 'intraday' (open loss counts). In-sample 2021-09-22 -> 2024-12-31.
**Verdict: nothing qualifies.** Best P(pass <= 5 days) of any config at any rules cell, in-sample: Flex 36%, Pro +DLL 41%, Pro noDLL 42%. Re-picked every quarter and tested on the next one (out of sample): Flex 34%, Pro +DLL 34%, Pro noDLL 37%. The bar is 60% and it is not relaxed. Configs with ANY rules cell at or above 60% on a Lucid account: 0 of 1,781.
Why: with open losses counted, an eval is 'make +$3,000 before you are ever down $2,000'. A strategy with no edge can do that at most 40% of the time (Flex about 33%: two days needed, no day over half the profit). The old 69-81% came from stops of several thousand dollars whose open loss the old model ignored (section 7).
**Past approved accounts: yes, affected.** Replayed tick by tick (the desk builds, take as a real exit): nq_nyam_flex passes 32% in-sample (33% on 2025-26, a second look), nq_nyam_pro 40% (41%), not the approved 78% / 81%. Section 7.

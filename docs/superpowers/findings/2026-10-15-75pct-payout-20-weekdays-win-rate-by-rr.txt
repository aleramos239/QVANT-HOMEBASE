HOW TO READ
- Trades/day = total trades across all strategies. The number of algos does not matter.
- More trades/day = slightly lower win rate needed, smaller risk per trade. 6-8 a day is the sweet spot.
- Risk/trade is about $150-300 at 6-8 trades a day. Bigger only if 4 or fewer.
- Low RR needs a high win rate. High RR needs a low win rate but a bigger edge per trade.
- Win rates move in 2.5% steps and the sim has noise: treat each row as +/- 2.5 points.
- Closest to what the Desk shows (52-60% win, RR near 1): the 1:1.25 and 1:1.5 rows at 6-8 trades/day.
  They need +0.26R to +0.35R per trade; the Desk makes about +0.09R today.

2. WIN RATE STABILITY IS STEEP
Recipe: 6 trades/day, 60% win, 1 : 1.25, $200 risk.

---------------------+------------+-------------
60% (as planned)     | 87%        | 90%

55%                  | 64%        | 76%
52.5%                | 50%        | 68%
50%                  | 35%        | 55%

- 5 points of win-rate drift costs about 20-25 points of max payout.
- A win rate that flips between 65% and 55% month to month (average 60%) still gives 82%.
  What hurts is a lower average, not a swingy one.
- Judge a strategy on its weak half, not its best month.

4. CONSISTENCY (the Lucid rules)
- Flex has no payout consistency rule. One big day is fine. Profit must reach $4,000 for max payout.
- LucidPro has a 40% largest-day rule. High-RR recipes (1:2, 1:2.5) get a few big days = riskier on Pro.
  Low-RR, high-win recipes are safer there.
- Worst day vs the trailing floor is the real check: a -6R day at $200 = -$1,200, over half the $2,000 floor.
  Keep worst day x risk under about 40% of the floor.

5. OTHERS WORTH TRACKING
- Losing streaks: the 20-day clock punishes a bad first week; the floor only trails up on gains.
- Open loss vs closed loss: Lucid counts open losses; deep intraday dips bust earlier than closed P&L shows.
- Trade count: Desk unseen stats rest on 60-300 trades per strategy. A 60% win rate on 100 trades is really ~50-70%.

RANKING A CANDIDATE
Daily Sharpe of the group > 0.5, win rate on the weak half of the data (not the average),
correlation to the rest of the portfolio < 0.3, worst day under 40% of the floor.
